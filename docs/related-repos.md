## Avoid

- **Auto-apply bots** (feder-cr/Jobs_Applier_AI_Agent_AIHawk, now redirected to an anti-bot-detection tool; GodsScion/Auto_job_applier_linkedIn; galiprandi/job-seeker; Anshul439/job-auto-apply; selfishprimate/seekter): they apply without your review and risk account bans.
- **Email finders that scrape or log into LinkedIn** (theHarvester, CrossLinked, linkedin2username, bhealy/linkedin): pentest tools that put your account at risk.
- **brilliant-cv's hidden keyword layer:** screening systems treat invisible keywords as gaming. The template itself is fine; don't turn that option on.
- **Salary scrapers for AmbitionBox or Levels.fyi:** all break those sites' terms. No open India designer salary dataset exists; use the pay ranges Ashby and FreeHire postings carry.
