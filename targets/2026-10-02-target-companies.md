# Target companies — tiered against the ICP

Checked 2 Oct 2026. Method: `tam-builder` (Tanyo Gochev) for the list rules, scored with the gates in `01-profile-and-icp.md`. Four parallel searches (dev tools/AI, fintech, B2B SaaS, design tools) swept roughly 2,000 company job boards on Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever and Workday, plus LinkedIn's public job search. Results were merged and deduplicated by company.

**How to read the tiers**

- **Tier 1** — passes every gate, and the role was posted or reposted in the last 30 days. Apply this week.
- **Tier 2** — fits, but the posting is 30+ days old, or one fact needs checking first. Apply or message, lower urgency.
- **Abroad** — split by whether the posting itself mentions visa or relocation help.
- **Stretch / under-level** — listed so you can decide, not recommended as a priority.

**Two things that apply to every row**

- **Pay is unverified unless the Pay column shows a figure.** Almost no posting states it. "—" means not stated.
- **Posting dates from LinkedIn can be reposts.** Where the original date is known, both are shown.

"Lead with" says which piece of your work to open the application with.
