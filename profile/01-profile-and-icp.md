## Part 2 — ICP matrix (which employers to pursue)

The rule from the skill: an employer is **out until it earns its way in**. Each gate is a yes/no question with a named source. Failing any gate excludes the company; a strong score elsewhere does not rescue it. Gates run cheapest first.

### Disqualifiers first

- Agencies, IT services, outsourcing, staffing firms.
- Turinglabs (current employer). L&T / LTIMindtree (you left it for product work).
- Roles asking 8+ years, or people-management roles.
- Roles abroad that require existing work authorisation and offer no sponsorship.
- Offers that cannot reach ₹25 LPA.
- Companies with no shipped product, or no designer and no founder who values design.

### The gates

| # | Gate | Pass if | Source to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Structural fit | Product company in one of: dev tools / AI products, fintech / investing, enterprise B2B SaaS, design tools. Any stage. Location is Bengaluru hybrid, remote India, global remote that hires in India, or abroad with sponsorship. | Company site, careers page, posting location line |
| 2 | Problem evidence | The posting or product shows at least one need you have proof for: a design system to build or scale, multi-brand or theming, data-heavy or financial workflows, motion and interaction quality, or a designer expected to prototype or ship in code. | Job description text, the product itself |
| 3 | Exclusions | None of the disqualifiers above apply. | Posting, company LinkedIn page |
| 4 | Reachability | There is an open role you can apply to, or a named head of design or founder you can message. | Careers page, LinkedIn people tab |
| 5 | Comp | The band can plausibly reach ₹30 LPA (floor ₹25 LPA), or the equivalent abroad. | Posting, Levels.fyi, AmbitionBox, Glassdoor, funding stage |
| 6 | Timing | A matching design role is live and was posted within about 30 days. Over 90 days is treated as filled or frozen. | Posting date |

### Tiers

- **Tier 1** — passes all six gates. Tailored application plus a direct note to the hiring manager, leading with the matching case study.
- **Tier 2** — passes gates 1–5, but no live role (or the posting is stale). Short note to the head of design; watch the careers page.
- **Tier 3** — passes gate 1 only. Follow, do not spend time.

### Which case study leads, by segment
