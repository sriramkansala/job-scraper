**Sources.** Every fact comes from `job-scraper/profile/01-profile-and-icp.md` and `job-scraper/profile/designer-profile.md`. Nothing else is assumed. Where an idea needs a moment only you know, the angle says **needs your story:** and names what's missing. If you can't fill one in truthfully, skip that idea.

**Skills followed.**
- `gali-firon/linkedin-ghostwriting`. I ran its anti-repeat gate at the level of the argument, not the wording. You have no posting history yet, so I checked each idea against the other 89, and every idea makes a different point. Ideas that use the same project are spaced apart in the schedule. Most formats carry an image, carousel, video or screenshot, because the skill rules out bare text posts. Where I chose "text post", add a screenshot if you have one. Keep each post to about six lines. End on a thought or a question, never on a plug.
- `gali-firon/anti-ai-slop-writing`. I ran both of its passes on every hook and angle. Pass one removed these: "it's not X, it's Y" lines, "Here's the thing" teasers, colon reveals, questions the post answers itself, "Think of it like" analogies, and invented numbers. Pass two avoided stacked buzzwords and em dashes. When you write the posts, run the same two passes, then add one or two real details of your own.
